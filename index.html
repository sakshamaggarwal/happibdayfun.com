What Are Birthday Freebies?

Birthday freebies are free items, discounts, and rewards that restaurants, cafes, retail stores, and beauty brands offer customers on or around their birthday. Many popular chains like Starbucks, Sephora, Denny's, Chipotle, and Krispy Kreme have birthday reward programs. Some require advance signup through a loyalty program, while others let you walk in and show your ID on the day.

How to Claim Birthday Freebies

There are three main types of birthday deals. Walk-in freebies require no signup — just show your ID at the restaurant. Same-day signup rewards let you download the app or join the loyalty program on your birthday and still get the freebie. Advance signup programs require you to register days or weeks before your birthday. Use the filters above to find which type works for you.
